Common questions

A video won't turn into a lesson. SayHo needs captions in the language you're learning. Videos with auto-generated captions in another language, or none at all, can't be used. Try another video, or start from Picks.

The microphone isn't working. Check Settings → SayHo → Microphone and Speech Recognition are both allowed.

Managing your subscription. Subscriptions are handled by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Deleting your account. In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and everything saved to it.
