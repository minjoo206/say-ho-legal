Privacy Policy

SayHo · Last updated 22 July 2026

SayHo helps you learn a language by shadowing — speaking along with real audio — and by saving vocabulary. This policy explains exactly what we collect, what stays on your device, and who else is involved.

Who we are

SayHo is operated by Iron Mind Studios ("we", "us"). You can reach us at minjoo@ironmindstudios.com.

What stays on your device

Some of the most sensitive things the app touches never reach us at all:

  • Your practice recordings. When you shadow a line, the app records you so you can play it back and compare. That audio is written to your device and is never uploaded to us. It is replaced by your next attempt.
  • Photos you scan for vocabulary. Text recognition runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The image itself is never uploaded. Only the recognised text is sent to our servers so we can pick out vocabulary from it.
  • App settings and preferences, such as your playback speed and whether sound effects are on.

You can use SayHo without an account. In that case a temporary anonymous session is created so your progress works; if you later sign in, that progress is linked to your account.

Microphone and speech recognition

To score your shadowing, the app transcribes what you say using Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device, language, and settings, Apple may process that audio on their servers rather than on the device. That processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive only the resulting text, and we do not store your audio.

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of providers to run the service:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account and learning data live here.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in. They tell us your email and an identifier; we do not receive your password.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates lesson content, vocabulary meanings, examples and grammar notes from text. Your audio and images are never sent.
  • ElevenLabs — converts text to speech so you can hear pronunciation.
  • YouTube — videos play through YouTube's official embedded player. YouTube may set cookies and collect data under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control that.
  • Apple — handles all subscription payments. We never see your card details.

Shared decks

If you choose to share a vocabulary folder, its name and cards are stored on our servers and can be retrieved by anyone who has the eight-character code you give them. Shared decks are not listed, searchable, or browsable — a deck can only be opened with its exact code.

You can stop sharing at any time from the folder's menu, which permanently disables that code. Note that anyone who already imported the deck keeps their own copy, because it became their words.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not upload your voice recordings or your photos.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from inside the app — open Settings and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and the learning data associated with it, and it cannot be undone.

Decks you published are removed when you revoke them or delete your account. Reports you submitted may be retained after deletion so we can act on them, since they concern other people's content.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. The in-app account deletion covers deletion directly; for anything else, contact us at minjoo@ironmindstudios.com and we will respond within the time your local law requires.

Children

SayHo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
